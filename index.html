How EduSpace works

Understand → Diagnose → Adapt → Explain

Four steps happen quietly behind every question a student answers. Click a card to see what EduSpace is doing at each stage.

01

Understand

Builds a live map of what a student already knows, topic by topic.

Every answer — right or wrong — updates a per-topic confidence score, not just a pass/fail mark.

02

Diagnose

Looks past the wrong answer to find the specific misconception behind it.

Two students can pick the same wrong option for different reasons. EduSpace tells those reasons apart.

03

Adapt

Adjusts difficulty and topic order so the next question meets the student where they are.

No fixed curriculum order — the path reshapes itself around weak topics as they're found.

04

Explain

Gives an explanation aimed at the actual misconception, not a generic hint.

The AI tutor addresses the specific gap it diagnosed, so the explanation actually closes it.

The hard part of personalization

Same wrong answer. Different reasons.

Two students answer the same question incorrectly. A simple system would give them the same correction. EduSpace doesn't.

A
Student A Topic: Fractions — Adding unlike denominators

3/4 + 1/2 = 4/6

What's actually happening

Adds numerators and denominators straight across — hasn't yet learned to find a common denominator.

What EduSpace does next

Shows a visual common-denominator walkthrough before the next fraction question.

vs
B
Student B Topic: Fractions — Adding unlike denominators

3/4 + 1/2 = 4/6

What's actually happening

Found a common denominator correctly, but made an arithmetic slip converting 1/2 to eighths.

What EduSpace does next

Skips the conceptual explainer — gives one quick practice conversion instead.

What's built in

Everything adapts around the student

01

Adaptive difficulty

Question difficulty rises and falls with demonstrated understanding, topic by topic, not as one blended score.

02

AI tutoring on mistakes

When a student gets something wrong, the tutor explains the specific misconception it detected — not a generic "try again."

03

Weak-topic tracking

A running map of where a student is confident and where they're not, visible to the student and updated after every answer.

04

Personalized pacing

No two students move through the same material at the same speed, or necessarily in the same order.

Our company

Built for the student behind the wrong answer

EduSpace started from a simple observation: most learning tools grade the answer and stop there. We think the more useful question is why the answer was wrong — and what a student needs next because of it. That's the problem we're building EduSpace to solve.
